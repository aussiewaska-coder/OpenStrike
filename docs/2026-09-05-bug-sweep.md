# Flight and visual bug sweep — 5 September 2026

Flight remains game-tuned assisted flight, with a compressed speed envelope;
these changes do not claim measured F-22 handling.

## Controls and flight

- Removed on-screen flight/camera sticks and automatic touch fallback. Android
  requires a gamepad and pauses when it disconnects. Screen target locking,
  radar interaction, settings, and the existing HUD layout fixes remain.
  Deleted touch scripts remain recoverable through Git; their pre-removal
  local versions are also saved in `/tmp/openstrike-touch-backup.3g1Olo`.
- L2 + R2 now engage vectoring, with pressure hysteresis. Equal pressure
  cancels rudder; a difference still commands yaw. R1 adjusts throttle without
  changing the manoeuvre envelope.
- Added side force opposing cross-body airflow. Previously rudder changed the
  nose much more than the path, then the yaw damper rapidly undid the heading
  change. Bounded release damping now gives the path time to follow the nose.
- Rudder authority now decreases with dynamic pressure. Corner speed is
  calculated from the load limit and lift curve instead of being rounded.
- The controller integration test applies actual input-service routing and
  aircraft physics. At 130 m/s, a three-second banked pull measures roughly
  367 m radius normally and 292 m with vectoring. Speed falls to about 122 and
  115 m/s respectively. These are transient manoeuvre measurements, not
  sustained turn ratings. A 1.5-second rudder pulse followed by three seconds
  of release settles onto about 19 degrees of changed track, formerly 4.

The side-force and yaw-moment distinction follows
[NASA's aircraft yaw explanation](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/VirtualAero/BottleRocket/airplane/yaw.html).
The F-22's two-dimensional nozzles are documented by the
[US Air Force](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104506/f-22-raptor/About-Us/f-22-raptor/).
The game's rudder remains aerodynamic; the trigger combination selects the
expanded pitch envelope.

## Visuals and other findings

- Corrected the lens flare's reversed sun direction.
- Reduced the first directional-shadow split from 650 m to 162.5 m while
  retaining the 6.5 km overall range. The rendered near-aircraft shadow is
  better defined. [Godot documents the distance/detail tradeoff](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_directionallight3d.html#class-directionallight3d-property-directional-shadow-split-1).
- Disabled physics interpolation on frame-driven cloud, rain, drone, and enemy
  jet transforms, matching the rendered camera and target positions. This
  follows [Godot's interpolation requirements](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/physics/interpolation/using_physics_interpolation.html).
- Updated targeting after the camera each frame; radar heading uses the
  displayed +X nose axis for both aircraft. The HUD reads actual jet load.
- Smoke breadcrumbs now fill every distance interval and interpolate their
  birth times, making spacing and ageing consistent across frame rates.
- Smoke geometry begins a mesh surface only when a visible quad exists,
  avoiding repeated empty-surface errors on the Compatibility renderer.
- Corrected the drone evasion cross-product sign.
- Fixed stale stall and bomb-drag assertions, moved scene-dependent fixtures
  into the initialized tree, freed test-owned nodes, and prevented edited
  tests from overwriting failure exits. The suite runner also rejects logged
  engine errors independently of exit status.

## Validation and limits

`GODOT_BIN=/path/to/godot tools/run_tests.sh` runs the complete headless suite.
All 62 tests passed, with engine errors treated as failures. The final shadow
setting and flight harness adjustment also passed their targeted rechecks.
`tests/flight_sweep_test.gd` covers trigger routing, rudder release, low-speed
authority, and banked vectoring. `tests/lighting_wiring_test.gd` reproduces the
flare and cloud interpolation faults. The smoke renderer regression also runs
with a real GL Compatibility renderer.

`tests/visual_smoke_test.gd` can export noon, dusk, and night frames with a real
renderer using `++ --visual-out=/tmp/openstrike-lighting` after the engine
arguments. Software-rendered checks validate wiring and shader operation;
phone GPU shadow shimmer, Bluetooth feel, and device frame times still need a
connected Android device. No device was attached during this sweep.
