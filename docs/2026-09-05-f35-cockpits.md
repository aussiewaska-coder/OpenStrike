# F-35 flight and per-aircraft cockpits

The build starts in the supplied F-35 Lightning II, with the authored gear-up
parts visible and gear-down struts, wheels and lights hidden. The aircraft
selector retains the F-22, F-117, F/A-18F and Apache. All three embedded PNGs
were checked for chunk CRC and complete zlib decoding before import, and their
Android imports are limited to 2048 pixels.

The mesh has broad carrier-variant proportions (157.29 length / 131.59 span
in model units). A 13.1 m span produces 15.66 m length, matching the F-35C
proportions in Lockheed Martin's published family specifications:
https://www.lockheedmartin.com/content/dam/lockheed-martin/aero/documents/F-35/F35FastFacts5_2020.pdf
The profile uses the project's existing game flight envelope, not a claimed
engineering simulation of the real aircraft.

One existing afterburner plume is attached at the measured nozzle centre
(-40.56, -1.62, 0) in model-root units, at a 0.45 m radius once installed.
The shared plume shader now uses blue and pale white shock cells without its
previous red/orange tail. Engine spool, throttle detent and crash cutoff are
unchanged. No nozzle or control-surface animation was added to the F-35, as
requested in the user's correction.

The cockpit regression came from replacing _measure_cockpit with an
unconditional nose-camera measurement for every jet. A test against the real
models reproduced cameras outside both the F-22 and F/A-18F cockpit bounds.
The camera now measures the named cockpit in aircraft coordinates, locates
the pilot using the established seat/eye fractions, and preserves the original
slight downward panel view. The F-117 explicitly sets has_cockpit=false and
keeps its unobstructed nose camera. Interpolation, look controls and flight
orientation still use the physical aircraft frame.

Validation covers the F-35's single plume, hidden landing gear, no wing
animation, mounts, 15-second flight and engine cutoff; cockpit locations for
all four jets; actual game startup; aircraft switching; camera interpolation
and rotation; airframe dimensions; F-22 effects; F/A-18F effects; and F-117 gear.
F-35 cruise finished at 897.51 m from a 900 m launch. Separate 15-second runs
reached 187.91 m/s at military power and 238.10 m/s in afterburner.

The real Compatibility render in tools/check_jet_cockpits.gd captures F-22,
F/A-18F and F-35 cockpit interiors plus an underside/rear F-35 gear/plume view.

The F-35 uses a 0.45 longitudinal cockpit fraction, selected from rendered
seat positions. The default 0.60 presses the camera against its forward canopy
bow, while positions aft of 0.35 clip into the seat. The chosen position shows
the authored display panel and a clear forward view. The F-22 retains its
original 0.60 seat fraction and downward panel framing.
